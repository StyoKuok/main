## Overview

A compact competition robot combining embedded line following with a pre-programmed arm sequence for collecting floor objects, pushing them into a target region, and placing objects on an elevated platform.

## My contribution

Integrated and tuned the chassis tracking, task sequence, and manipulator motion on the physical field.

## Result

**Zhongkong Cup Robotics Competition · Second Prize.**
